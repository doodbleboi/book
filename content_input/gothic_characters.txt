  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Noemí Taboada</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Protagonist</span>
    <p>A smart, elegant 22-year-old socialite and anthropology student from Mexico City. She loves stylish dresses, driving her convertible, and smoking cigarettes. While her father views her as a party girl, her sharp mind, bravery, and stubborn independence make her the ultimate threat to the Doyle family's patriarchal control.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Catalina Sandoval Doyle</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">The Imperiled Cousin</span>
    <p>Noemí's gentle, orphaned cousin whose frantic letter sets the story in motion. Swept into a whirlwind romance with Virgil Doyle, she finds herself trapped at High Place, drugged by the family physician, and fighting for her sanity against the fungal whispers in the walls.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Francis Doyle</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Ally / Confidant</span>
    <p>The quiet, sickly son of Florence Doyle. Unlike his arrogant relatives, Francis is soft-spoken, studies botany and photography, and shows real empathy. Torn between obedience to his family and his growing affection for Noemí, he ultimately risks his life to help the cousins destroy High Place.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Virgil Doyle</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Antagonist / Husband</span>
    <p>Catalina's charming yet predatory husband. Arrogant and calculating, he manages High Place with rigid authority. He attempts to gaslight Noemí into believing Catalina is insane and uses the fungal spores to manipulate and violate Noemí's will.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Howard Doyle</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">The Ancient Patriarch</span>
    <p>The decrepit, centuries-old founder of the Doyle dynasty. Obsessed with Victorian eugenics, skull measurements, and racial purity, he extends his life by consuming an ancient European fungus that binds his descendants into a shared hive mind through incestuous marriages.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Florence Doyle</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Household Enforcer</span>
    <p>Francis's rigid, unsmiling mother who runs High Place like a prison. She enforces strict rules against speaking Spanish, playing music, or leaving the estate without permission, fanatically dedicated to preserving the family's dying bloodline.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Dr. Arthur Cummins</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Complicit Physician</span>
    <p>The local English doctor loyal to the Doyles. He dismisses Catalina's and Noemí's deteriorating health as female hysteria while administering sedatives that keep them weakened and trapped inside the mansion.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Marta Duval</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Village Curandera</span>
    <p>A respected folk healer in the nearby town of El Triunfo. Having witnessed decades of local miners suffering under the Doyles, she provides Noemí with herbal remedies that help break the fungal mind-control of the Gloom.</p>
