  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Anna Klein</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Protagonist / Translator</span>
    <p>A resilient Austrian refugee living in one room at Adolfsallee 45. She starts her mornings eating a spoonful of lard and brushing her teeth with her finger while nursing a wobbly tooth. Formerly an apprentice at her uncle's art gallery in Salzburg, she speaks fluent English. Wearing dead Uncle Otto's oversized pinstripe wool pants and wristwatch, she steals an Army jeep, faces down Nazi art thieves, and rejects returning to her communist husband in the Soviet zone to build a free life with Amalia and Oskar.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Captain Henry "Coop" Cooper</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">MFAA Arts Officer</span>
    <p>An architect from Chicago who grew up on an Iowa farm. He volunteered for the Monuments Men to save historic buildings and art across Europe. Armed with a cracked Kodak 35 camera, a pack of chewing gum, and an upbeat American attitude, he hates military red tape. Even after Major Phillips exiles him to count supplies at Erbenheim airfield, Cooper stands by Anna and secretly opens the wooden crate of Queen Nefertiti for the children.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Amalia Klein</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Anna's 6-Year-Old Daughter</span>
    <p>A clever girl who wears mismatched socks inside dusty shoes with the toes cut off. She carries Lulu, a dirty doll missing an eye and hair, and wears a heart-shaped metal dirndl button on a green silk ribbon. She proudly wears her apartment door key on a red ribbon like a medal. Her love for Hans Christian Andersen's <em>The Snow Queen</em> and her discovery of Frieda's hidden SS pin crack the whole mystery wide open.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Oskar Grünewald</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Key Witness / Orphan</span>
    <p>An eight-year-old orphan found hiding in an upstairs wardrobe at the Dotzheim villa with a pocket knife, a bottle opener, and a pencil. Adopted through the SS <em>Lebensborn</em> home in Steinhöring, he survived the fiery September 1944 bombing of Darmstadt beneath his dying mother Magda. Forced by his uncle to guard stolen paintings, he is the boy shepherd in his mother's Rhine landscape and finds a real home with Anna.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Gerhart Heinrich ("Konrad Schenk")</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Primary Antagonist</span>
    <p>The former owner of Galerie Neustadt in Mainz and a top art looter for the Nazi ERR. He faked his death certificate and stole the identity of dead restorer Konrad Schenk. He abandoned his nephew Oskar at the villa to guard his loot, bribed Major Phillips with Runge's stolen masterwork, and tried to extort $300 from Anna before being exposed by his dead sister's signature.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Ludwig Schneider</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Collaborator / Art Dealer</span>
    <p>A sweating, nervous former SA member and art dealer from Frankfurt. He helped steal Jewish art collections, tore off original gallery labels, and claimed the Dotzheim villa repository was his late wife's private property. He tried to bribe Anna with a basket of food and American cigarettes. In the end, MPs handcuff him and drag him out of the museum workshop.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Emil Schilling</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Tragic Wehrmacht Veteran</span>
    <p>A 22-year-old soldier who lost three fingers to frostbite at Leningrad. Haunted by nightmares of an SS executioner shooting a mother and baby in a Ukrainian field, he drinks Nordhäuser schnapps to quiet his mind. He stole the Runge painting and Cooper's Colt .45 to stop Heinrich. After stabbing his sister Frieda, he hangs himself in the Albrechtstrasse jail, leaving Anna the camera photo hidden behind his canvas.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Frieda Schilling</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Antagonist / Lebensborn Nurse</span>
    <p>Emil's tall sister who watches neighborhood children at her Gustav-Freytag-Strasse home, serving apple strudel and Nescafé. Beneath her pleasant act, she remains a fanatic Nazi who served as an SS <em>Helferin</em> and <em>Lebensborn</em> breeder at the Dotzheim villa. When Amalia finds her double-lightning-bolt pin, Frieda strikes the girl, provoking the violent fight that leaves her stabbed in the throat.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="dossier-card">
    <h4>Thomas Klein</h4>
    <span class="dossier-badge">Anna's Husband</span>
    <p>An idealistic Austrian doctor who survived water torture and knocked-out teeth from the Gestapo. Instead of fleeing west with his family, he stayed behind in Kappellendorf to serve the Soviet Kommandant. His final letter, boasting of running a Soviet hospital and eating fresh horsemeat, proves to Anna that politics matter more to him than his family, sealing their separation forever.</p>
  </div>
